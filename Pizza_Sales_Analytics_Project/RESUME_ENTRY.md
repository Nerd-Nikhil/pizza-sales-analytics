**Pizza Sales Analytics | Python, Pandas, SQL (MySQL), Excel, Power BI**
Dataset: 48,620 order lines, 21,350 orders (Kaggle)
• Cleaned and validated 48,620 order lines with Pandas (date parsing, price reconciliation, duplicate checks) and computed revenue, order-value and basket KPIs ($817.9K revenue, $38.31 average order value).
• Analysed hourly, weekday and monthly demand and product mix, finding a 12:00 to 13:59 lunch peak (23% of orders) and that 10 of 32 pizzas drive 44.5% of revenue.
• Wrote MySQL queries (distinct counts, window functions, UNION top/bottom-N) and reconciled results across Python, SQL and Excel.
• Built a formula-driven Excel dashboard with 7 charts and designed a Power BI report with DAX measures.
